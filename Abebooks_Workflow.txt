Abebooks Seller Account Workflow

1. Pre-Registration Protocol
- Dedicated Email Address: Establish a new, dedicated Gmail address intended for access to AbeBooks and sharing with us.
- Unique Phone Number: Use a US SIM card phone number starting in +1 that has no prior order history with either Amazon or AbeBooks.

2. Authentication and Account Security Configurations
- Store Name: Apply a unique Store Name to the AbeBooks seller account.
- Password: Apply a unique password to the AbeBooks seller account to facilitate shared management.

3. Seller Account Verification Timeline (1 week)
- AbeBooks Protocol: Seller applications require manual verification and approval by the AbeBooks Trust and Safety team, which typically takes 1 to 2 business days.
- Processing Window: Expect a standard processing time of 2 to 3 business days for approval of the seller account.

4. Privacy and Data Security
- Please be assured that we respect your data privacy and have no long-term requirement or interest in retaining personal details.
- Personal and financial information is used only to secure the platform's initial account approval.
- Immediately following account activation, we will update the registration profile by replacing all personal and financial data with our own corporate data.

5. Financial Routing and Card Validation
- We strongly recommend using a new, single-purpose Wise account for all Abebooks transactions.
- A new physical card requires a one-time setup fee of $10.00, while virtual cards can be generated instantly at no cost.
- The Wise platform charges $0.00 in ongoing monthly subscription or account maintenance fees.

6. Handover and Delivery Protocol (Scope: 6 days)
- Credential Access: Share the login credentials for the newly created Gmail address.
- Platform Access: Share the login credentials for the AbeBooks seller account.
- Delivery Submission: Initiate and submit the formal order delivery via the Fiverr platform.

7. Risk Assessment
- We will securely access the seller profile to manually list an initial sample of 3-4 book titles.
- The newly registered seller account will undergo a mandatory 24-hour verification period to check for operational flags.
- We closely monitor the system to ensure the listings do not trigger automatic security restrictions, policy updates, or account health warnings.

8. Final Order Approval
- If the seller profile remains completely stable and free of safety triggers by the following Tuesday, we will approve and finalize the delivery of your order on Fiverr.
- Upon successful order approval, we will submit an excellent service review to highlight your cooperation on the Fiverr platform.

9. Compliance Monitoring
- Once the account transition is complete, our team assumes full responsibility for daily operations under a managed-service structure.
- Please maintain an active communication status on the Fiverr platform so we can swiftly coordinate and resolve any issues with the Abebooks platform.
